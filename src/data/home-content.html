ARQIA is a luxury design studio where architecture, interiors, and engineering come together as one seamless discipline — shaping residences across Brazil and the United States with clarity, precision, and quiet sophistication.

We begin with space — how light moves, how volumes breathe, how a home connects to its surroundings. From that foundation, every detail is carefully composed, creating environments that feel effortless, balanced, and deeply intentional.

Our work is defined by refinement. Sculptural forms, curated materials, and a restrained sense of luxury that reveals itself over time. Nothing is excessive — yet everything is considered.

Through our global sourcing network, we bring together rare materials, innovative systems, and distinctive design elements from around the world — elevating each project beyond the expected.

At ARQIA, design is not layered. It is integrated. Architecture, interiors, and technical execution are conceived as one — resulting in spaces that are cohesive, timeless, and quietly powerful.

For those who value beauty with intelligence, and luxury with meaning, ARQIA delivers more than a project — we create a complete living experience.
